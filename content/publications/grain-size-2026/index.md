abstract: >
  We systematically investigate which aspects of dust grain size evolution
  leave a detectable imprint on galaxy observables, and which do not.
  Using cosmological hydrodynamic simulations, we identify the physical
  processes that most strongly influence the grain size distribution and
  assess their impact on the predicted spectral energy distributions of galaxies.

  We use cosmological simulations to disentangle the essential from the negligible.
