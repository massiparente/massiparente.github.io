  We investigate the physical drivers behind the evolution of cosmic dust temperature
  across galaxy populations and cosmic time. Using cosmological simulations and
  semi-analytic models, we identify the key processes that set the dust temperature
  distribution in galaxies from the local universe to high redshift.
