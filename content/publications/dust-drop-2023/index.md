abstract: >
  Observations show a sharp decline in the cosmic dust mass density below z ∼ 1,
  yet its origin remains debated. We investigate this trend within a semi-analytic
  model of galaxy evolution, tracing the interplay between dust production,
  grain growth in the ISM, and dust destruction by supernova shocks and astration.
summary: >
  Why does cosmic dust abundance drop sharply at z ≲ 1? We use a semi-analytic
  model to identify the dominant mechanisms driving the low-redshift drop.
